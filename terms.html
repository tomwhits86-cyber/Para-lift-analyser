2. What RefLight provides

RefLight is an AI-powered video analysis tool designed to help para powerlifting athletes and coaches assess bench press technique against World Para Powerlifting (WPP) technical rules.

The service provides:

  • AI-generated analysis of uploaded training videos
  • Assessment of technique against WPP rule criteria
  • Referee risk assessment indicating the likelihood of technical rule compliance
  • Coaching cues and technical corrections
  • AI velocity estimation (uncalibrated directional indicator)
  • Exportable PDF reports

RefLight is a coaching and preparation aid. It is not a substitute for qualified coaching, official referee assessment, or professional sports science advice.

3. No guarantee of competition outcomes

Important: RefLight does not guarantee, predict, or promise any specific outcome at competition. All analysis represents an assessment of likelihood and risk based on the information visible in the uploaded video. Actual competition results depend on many factors outside RefLight's assessment, including referee discretion, competition conditions, and factors not visible in training footage.

Specifically:

  • A "Green" or positive verdict does not guarantee white lights in competition
  • A "Warning" or "Amber" verdict does not guarantee red lights in competition
  • Referee decisions are made by qualified human officials and RefLight's analysis cannot replicate or replace that judgement
  • Analysis accuracy depends on video quality, camera angle, and the visibility of key technical moments in the uploaded footage
  • Velocity estimates are AI visual estimates and should not be treated as calibrated measurements

RefLight's assessments should be used as one input among several in an athlete's competition preparation — not as a definitive prediction of competition results.
